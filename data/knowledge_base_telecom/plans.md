# Mobile Plans & Pricing

## Unlimited Plans

**Basic Unlimited** — $50/month
- Unlimited talk & text
- 5 GB high-speed data, then throttled
- North America coverage

**Premium Unlimited** — $70/month
- Unlimited talk & text
- Unlimited data, no throttling
- International roaming ($5/day)

**Family Unlimited** — $120/month (2 lines)
- Unlimited everything per line
- Hotspot included
- Parental controls

## Pay-As-You-Go

**Pay Per Use** — $0.10/min talk, $0.05/text, $0.01/MB data
- No monthly commitment
- Auto-replenish credits

## Data Add-Ons

- 1 GB: $10
- 5 GB: $30
- Unlimited: $40/month

## Device Financing

Devices available on monthly installments (24 months, 0% APR):
- iPhone 15: $30/month

**Note:** Prices exclude taxes and fees. Early termination fee applies if you cancel before 24 months.
