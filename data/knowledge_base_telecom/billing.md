# Billing & Payment

## Your Monthly Bill

Your bill is generated on the same day each month (your billing date). It includes:

- Plan charges
- Device payments
- Add-ons (data, international, etc.)
- Taxes and regulatory fees
- Prorated charges (if you made mid-cycle changes)

## How to Pay

1. **Online**: Sign into my.telecom.com and select "Pay Now"
2. **Mobile App**: Open app > Billing > Pay Now
3. **Automatic Payment**: Set up AutoPay to avoid late fees
4. **Phone**: Call 1-800-TELECOM
5. **Mail**: Send check to: Telecom Inc., PO Box 123, City, ST 12345

## Payment Methods

- Credit/debit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)
- Bank account (ACH)
- Check

## Late Fees & Disconnection

- **Late fee:** $25 (applied 15 days after due date)
- **Service disconnection:** 30 days after due date
- **Reconnection fee:** $50

## Billing Disputes

If you believe there's an error on your bill:
1. Call 1-800-TELECOM within 60 days
2. Provide billing cycle and details
3. Dispute investigation takes 5-7 business days

**Note:** You are not required to pay disputed amounts during investigation.
