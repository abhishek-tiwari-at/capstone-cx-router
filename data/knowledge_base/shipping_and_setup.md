# Shipping and Product Setup

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days; express takes 1-2. A tracking link is
emailed once an order ships. For device setup, first fully charge the device,
then download the companion app and follow the in-app pairing steps. If a device
will not power on, hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a restart before
troubleshooting further. Firmware updates install automatically when the device
is connected to Wi-Fi.
