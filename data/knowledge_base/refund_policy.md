# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase for most products.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically take 5-7 business
days to appear. Opened consumable items and gift cards are non-refundable.
Digital purchases are refundable within 14 days if the content has not been
downloaded. To start a refund, the customer's order must be located by order ID
or the email on the account.
