# Billing FAQ

Charges appear on statements under the merchant name "CX-STORE". A pending
authorization may show a temporary hold that clears in 1-3 days. Customers can
update their payment method under Account > Billing. If a customer sees a
duplicate charge, one of the two is usually a pending authorization that will
drop off; if both settle, it qualifies for a refund review. Invoices and receipts
can be downloaded from the Billing History page.
