# How to Check Your Account Balance

You can check your account balance in several ways:

1. **Online Banking Portal**: Log into your online banking account at banking.example.com. Your current balance is displayed on the dashboard.

2. **Mobile App**: Open the official bank app, go to "Accounts", and select the account you want to check.

3. **Phone**: Call our automated customer service at 1-800-BANK-123 and follow the prompts.

4. **ATM**: Insert your debit card at any of our ATMs and select "Check Balance".

**Note:** Pending transactions may not be reflected immediately. Your available balance may differ from your current balance.

**Contact Support**: If you cannot access your balance or notice an error, call us at 1-800-BANK-123.
