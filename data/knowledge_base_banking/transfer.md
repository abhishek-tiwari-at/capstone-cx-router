# How to Transfer Money

## Internal Transfers (Between Your Accounts)

1. Log into online banking
2. Select "Transfer Funds"
3. Choose source and destination accounts
4. Enter amount
5. Confirm and submit

Transfers between your own accounts are typically instant or process within 1 business day.

## External Transfers (To Another Bank)

For transfers to accounts at other banks, we use ACH (Automated Clearing House) which takes **1-3 business days**.

**Limits:**
- Daily: $5,000
- Monthly: $25,000

## Wire Transfers

For urgent transfers, wire transfers process **same-day** (if submitted before 3 PM ET).

**Fees:** $25 per wire transfer

**Required Information:**
- Recipient's full name
- Bank routing number
- Account number
- Amount

**Note:** Wire transfers are non-reversible, so verify recipient details before sending.
